About Pr0 Social

Artist Bio

Jerrell Lanos

Rapper · Singer · DJ — Buffalo, NY

Pr0 Social is a rapper and singer from Buffalo, NY (originally from Rochester) who blends contemporary R&B with witty rap lyrics. The 29-year-old artist, whose real name is Jerrell Lanos, draws inspiration from artists like D'Angelo, Miguel, Frank Ocean, and Kendrick Lamar.

What sets Pr0 Social apart is his refusal to be boxed in. His debut mixtape Adience (2018) introduced listeners to a voice unafraid of vulnerability, while his collaborative album Frequencies with fellow Buffalo artist Jedi pushed the sonic boundaries further. His debut album In Due Time (2019) explored heavy themes — race, family, love, money, loyalty, and mental health — with a candor rarely heard in the genre.

2023's Things I've Buried marked a breakthrough moment, accumulating over 300,000 streams across all platforms and earning a feature from Jae Skeese, an artist from Conway the Machine's label. Three tracks from that album — "promises," "chainsmoking," and "still" — were featured in an NPR podcast series exploring a Buffalo cheerleading squad's resilience in the aftermath of the May 2022 Tops Friendly Markets shooting. That recognition cemented Pr0 Social's place not just as a regional talent, but as a storyteller with national resonance.

In February 2025, he returned with the rap-focused EP some don't make it here… — a direct, unflinching look at survival, community, and perseverance. Now venturing into EDM and Bass DJing, Pr0 Social continues to evolve, with an upcoming all-R&B project already generating anticipation.

He has toured in Richmond, Boston, Toronto, and Pennsylvania, and has headlined major Buffalo venues and festivals including Town Ballroom, Beau Fleuve, Porch Fest, and MiA Festival.
