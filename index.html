About the Artist

Where R&B Meets Raw Truth

Pr0 Social is a rapper, singer, and DJ from Buffalo, NY whose music blends contemporary R&B with witty rap lyrics. Inspired by D'Angelo, Miguel, Frank Ocean, and Kendrick Lamar, Jerrell Lanos crafts introspective, genre-defying music that speaks to race, family, love, and mental health.

With over 300,000+ streams across platforms, NPR recognition, and sold-out Buffalo shows, Pr0 Social continues to grow as one of the city's most compelling voices.
